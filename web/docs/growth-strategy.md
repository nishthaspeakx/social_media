## Measurement policy

- **availabilityStates**: ["available", "not_supported", "not_connected", "permission_missing", "delayed", "insufficient_sample", "not_applicable"]
- **missingValue**: null, never0; real measured0 remains0
- **cohorts**: Compare same platform, format, observation age, organic status and similar duration; mature at72h for operational view,7days for decisions; evergreen search also30days.
- **baseline**: At least10mature comparable posts before a median index; median0 disables ratio. Index100 is own baseline, not industry quality.
- **snapshots**: ["1h", "6h", "24h", "72h", "7d", "30d"]
- **crossPlatform**: Show platform panels; sum counts only as gross events with duplicate-audience warning, never unique total reach.
- **index**: primary_metric / matched_cohort_median ×100;130/70 triage thresholds are configurable heuristics, not significance.
- **privacy**: Keep only consented first-party identifiers; suppress small demographic groups per source constraints.
