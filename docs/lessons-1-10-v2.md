## Keeping Sia's mouth natural

- **Use a relaxed, closed-mouth reference photo.** Kling builds every frame from the reference, so a laughing photo makes the mouth open too wide on every word. The office photo catches Sia mid-laugh. The app now labels it “may exaggerate lip-sync” and labels the front view “best for lip-sync”.
- **A portrait (9:16), chest-up photo is best.** The current photos are landscape, so the model makes landscape video that has to be cropped and enlarged about 3.5×. That softens and stretches the face.
- **Every prompt below includes a natural-mouth rule.** The mouth opens only as much as each syllable needs, closes between phrases, and shows a soft closed-lip smile. Energy comes from eyebrows, head and hands instead of the mouth.
