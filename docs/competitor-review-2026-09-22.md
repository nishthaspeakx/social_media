# SpeakX competitor review — 22 September 2026

Chrome inspection used Meta Ads Library, India, active commercial ads. Seekho, Stimuler and Clapingo were selected through their advertiser pages. ELSA and Stimuler official product pages supplied additional positioning evidence. Detailed ad IDs, observed dates, durations, source links and limitations are preserved in `web/strategy.json` and displayed in Competitor intelligence.

## Recommended tests, in order

1. Specific speaking problem → usable sentence within the first few seconds → viewer practice. Test an interview hesitation hook against a direct two-line introduction hook.
2. One language error → correct sentence → try again. Compare a question opener with a neutral lesson opener.
3. Warm, judgment-free encouragement → a low-pressure speaking turn. Keep Sia as the sole presenter; no fabricated learner testimonials.
4. One useful result per daily episode. Build recurring Sia familiarity without requiring a map or location in every post.

Seekho repeatedly uses Hindi/regional-language messages about useful learning and English confidence. Its 89-second English ad (3657870504382069) opened with a human-faced robot at a microphone. This is an attention device, not a reason to change Sia's identity. Stimuler repeats speaking-hesitation and feedback messages, uses creator partnerships, and shows variants of similar copy. Its 59-second ad (1373260911320164) opened with a close-up creator, microphone and prominent text. Clapingo's five-card sampled page uses reassurance, one-to-one practice and a historical ₹99 trial message; the inspected cards were explicitly labeled low impression count (<100).

These are observations and editorial hypotheses. Active dates do not prove continuous spend or profitable results; visible counts are search results, not audited unique creative totals. No CTR, CPA, ROAS or retention data was available. Paid creative mechanisms must be validated on SpeakX's organic accounts.

## Evaluation

Change one variable in matched hook tests. Keep platform, duration band, lesson difficulty, presenter, CTA and observation window comparable. Check 24-hour, 72-hour and seven-day outcomes. Require at least three comparable posts per variant; mark sparse or inconsistent evidence inconclusive. Assess early hold with platform-native definitions, then completion/APV, saves per reach, follow conversion and verified app activation. Never pool unlike denominators across platforms. Paid boosting remains a later decision requiring real organic results.
