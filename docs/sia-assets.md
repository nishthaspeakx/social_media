# Sia reference library

Four owner-supplied screenshots are preserved byte-for-byte in `web/assets/sia/`. `library.json` records their dimensions, original filenames and SHA-256 checksums. The live library is under **Sia & assets**; the video editor’s Generate tab offers the three single-person sources through **Saved Sia reference**.

- **sia-front.png:** direct-to-camera identity and first voice test.
- **sia-profile.png:** side-angle continuity and listening direction.
- **sia-office.png:** expressive office, meeting or interview opening.
- **sia-expressions.png:** acting direction only. The three views must never enter a single-person video as one input frame.

The new off-white long-sleeve top and blue jeans are approved reference clothing alongside the earlier grey mock-neck studio outfit. Match the selected frame throughout a video. Keep Sia’s face, hair, skin tone and proportions consistent. Only Sia appears or speaks; counterparts can appear as text.

The owner permits background changes per video. Studio, office, café, interview and other lesson settings may use different backgrounds, lighting, props and framing. Maps are optional according to the lesson. These screenshots are opaque, including the black backgrounds; they are not transparent cutouts. Scene preparation must remove/replace the background and set the desired vertical framing before final production. This update saves and selects references; it does not automatically edit their backgrounds.

Selecting a library source copies it through the existing authenticated reference upload endpoint into private media storage, clears frame approval, and updates draft outfit guidance. Review the frame and save a new version before generation. Existing immutable versions stay intact. Commercial use rights were confirmed by the owner; the approved Cartesia voice remains required.
